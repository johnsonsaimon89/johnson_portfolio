Countries Filmed in

I’m an award-winning Tanzanian filmmaker passionate about telling authentic, human-centered stories. Since 2016, I’ve had the privilege of creating films across 24 countries, working as a director, DOP, and producer, working in diverse locations, creating films in diverse locations, from the East African savannah to the Amazon Rainforest and the Himalayan peaks.

My focus is on non-extractive filmmaking with Indigenous communities, highlighting humanity's bond with nature while preserving cultural and ecological diversity.

I’m also the founder of the African School of Storytelling (AFRISOS), where I mentor emerging African filmmakers and help them share their stories with the world.
